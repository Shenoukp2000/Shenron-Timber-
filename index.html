About the company

Six estates in the Sabaragamuwa hill country

අපගේ වතු හය எங்கள் ஆறு தோட்டங்கள்

Shenron Timber operates across the wet-zone plantations of the Ratnapura district — high-rainfall hill country where Acacia mangium and Eucalyptus grow fast and dense. We purchase standing timber and hold the harvesting rights, managing felling, extraction, and haulage from estate to buyer.

Our resource base spans six estates under Hapugastenne Plantations PLC, a Browns Group company — giving buyers a single counterparty with plantation-scale volume and documented provenance.

Export logistics

Ratnapura to South India, door to port

A short corridor across the Palk Strait puts our estates within easy reach of the Kochi–Perumbavoor and Erode–Coimbatore timber clusters.

  1. Harvest & enumeration

    Felling under estate supervision with tree-by-tree enumeration records for your consignment.

  2. Depot & weighbridge

    Logs hauled to roadside depots; biomass weighed over certified weighbridges and sold by the metric tonne.

  3. Documentation

    VAT invoicing, transport permits, and phytosanitary / export paperwork prepared per consignment.

  4. Port & shipping

    Containerised or break-bulk loading ex-Colombo for Tuticorin, Kochi, and the Kerala–Tamil Nadu timber clusters.

Delivery terms: Ex-depot is our standard basis. FOB Colombo and CIF to your port can be quoted on request — state your preference in the RFQ.
